# Brag Plan: Kitten Medical Imaging AI Workstation

## What is this app?
Kitten is a next-generation diagnostic radiology copilot and PACS workstation that ingests raw DICOMs, performs real-time YOLO11n fracture localization, generates structured MedGemma clinical reports, and exports signed clinical PDFs in seconds.

## The angle
"Turn any raw DICOM from your hospital PACS into a clinically structured, AI-verified PDF report in under 5 seconds."
Medical AI tools usually force doctors through clunky interfaces, raw JSON outputs, or slow cloud workflows. Kitten proves what modern clinical imaging should feel like: seamless PACS connectivity, instant dual-engine AI diagnosis (YOLO11n + MedGemma), and 1-click clinical report export in a razor-sharp modern workstation.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
High-contrast dark clinical canvas with a pulsing Kitten rust beacon (`#8D321F`). A PACS incoming feed alert flashes: "PACS DICOM INGEST: Wrist AP/Lateral [STAT]". The workstation locks on.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Beat 1: Real-time YOLO11n Fracture Detection**: The raw X-ray renders in the Cornerstone3D viewport; AI bounding box snaps onto a distal radius fracture with confidence 0.94 and coordinates instantly tagged.
- **Beat 2: MedGemma Multimodal Clinical Findings**: Raw DICOM is synthesized into structured medical sections (Examination, Clinical Indication, Findings, Impression, Recommendations) with verified AI trust badges.
- **Beat 3: 1-Click Save as PDF Clinical Report**: Instant transition to a formatted, doctor-ready PDF with institutional header, annotated scan preview, and timestamped signature.

## Outro / punchline
"From Raw DICOM to Doctor-Ready PDF in 5 Seconds.
Kitten Diagnostic Radiology Copilot."
Kitten Burgundy Logo slam with live workstation status and CTA.

## User flow worth showing
Entry: Ingest DICOM from PACS feed → Key Action: Dual-engine AI scan (YOLO11n fracture box + MedGemma structured report generation) → Result: Instant 1-click export to clinical PDF report ready for doctor sign-off.
